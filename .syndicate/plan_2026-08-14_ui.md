## The premise correction

The audit falsifies the brief's own assumption, and everything below is ordered
around that. MLB is not the standard on any measured axis: worst card-height
spread (321 px desktop, 796 px mobile — five times the next sport), most type
combinations on one card, smallest tab labels (9 px) and touch targets (60×28),
no tabular figures. And `/mlb/cards` does not render the template other sports
were told to converge on — it renders `cards_source.js`, 3,880 lines, which
nothing can share.

The de facto standard is `_game_card_generic.html` + `game_board_contract`:
four sports, one view model, and §9's config sketch expresses every legitimate
MLB difference. **So MLB converges onto the generic path, not the reverse — and
it goes last**, because it carries 86% of artifact bytes and most traffic, and
because none of the user-visible defects are on it.

**Correction to the audit's Step 0:** it blocks the *consolidation* (Lane I), not
the fixes. Lanes E–H are all on the non-MLB path and need no product decision.
Do not let the renderer question gate an afternoon of work.

## Lane F — absent renders as absent

**This is one defect class in two layers, not two bugs.**
`game_board_contract.py:303-304` defaults missing win probabilities to `50.0`;
the model lane's `_fair_probability` defaults to `0.5`. Both render "no data" as
a confident neutral. Fix them together or the rule won't hold.

- **F1.** Kill the 50/50 default. An absent probability renders as absent — no
  centred bar. **Coordinate with model plan Lane A1**, same class, same week.
- **F2.** Give the probability bar a draw segment. Soccer currently shows
  `HOME WIN 77.3%` in the tiles and `Home win 81.1%` in the bar, ~250 px apart on
  one card. `game_board_contract` emits only `away_pct`/`home_pct`; there is no
  `draw` slot in any card template. Until F2 lands, the bar should be suppressed
  on three-way markets rather than showing a two-way number.
- **F3.** One null placeholder, applied at the contract. Pick `—`. Today: `-` on
  NCAAF (11 sites), empty on the generic card (Jinja silent-undefined), `-` in
  MLB's JS. Kill the empty-render path.
- **F4.** Grep the codebase for the shape — absent value substituted with a
  neutral midpoint — and write the rule into the engineer agent's notes. This
  has now appeared independently in the model layer and the view contract; treat
  it as a house pattern to be prevented, not two bugs to be fixed.

**Exit:** no surface renders a fabricated neutral for missing data; one
placeholder platform-wide; soccer shows one home-win number.

## Lane G — soccer end-to-end

Soccer is the worst surface on **both** audits simultaneously: 90 of 200
published shortlist rows, **zero** with a model comparison, two conflicting
home-win numbers, no draw slot, 13 px team names (vs 16 px on NFL), link-blue
team names on the card head, the projected-score sentence repeated four times on
one card, and 28 px overflow. It is also the largest published volume.

If one sport gets fixed end to end, the evidence says soccer, not MLB.

- **G1.** Team names: restyle the anchor to `--cards-text`; raise 13 px to match
  the 16 px used elsewhere.
- **G2.** De-duplicate the projected-score sentence (summary, Full Game card,
  twice under Top Plays).
- **G3.** Empty game-lens region — an MLB-shaped slot with no soccer data.
  Suppress it (see Lane I rule 2) rather than rendering blank area.
- **G4.** Cross-plan: soccer pregame is first in the model plan's archive-replay
  order. Run G alongside it so the sport gets fixed as a whole rather than in
  two disconnected passes.

## Lane I — consolidation (gated, MLB last)

Do not start before E and F land. Order within the lane matters: every step
before the last one is reversible.

- **I1.** One token file. The `--cards-*` palette is declared four times
  (`shared/app.css` 13, `mlb/cards_exact.css` 19, `nba/cards_source.css` 24,
  `wnba/cards-parity.css` 24) with **agreeing values but differing sets**.
  Agreement is what makes this safe to do first.
- **I2.** Define `--cards-surface-alt`, `--cards-text-muted`,
  `--cards-text-strong` in the token source and drop the Tailwind-slate
  fallbacks. Nine rules on every board page are currently outside the token
  system and will not respond to a palette change. Contrast passes either way
  (6.25:1) — this is consistency, not accessibility.
- **I3.** `wnba/cards-parity.css` + `nba/cards_source.css` → one
  `basketball_cards.css`. 97.4% identical, WNBA a strict subset missing 99
  live-lens-confidence lines. Do not backport the 99 lines into a fork that is
  about to be deleted.
- **I4.** `_scoreboard_strip_{mlb,soccer,generic}.html` → one.
- **I5.** `_game_card_{mlb,ncaaf,generic}.html` → one template plus sport
  config, per §9. The three differ in *which panels exist* and *what tiles are
  called*, not in structure.
- **I6.** MLB last: retire `cards_source.js` to `?client=source` and serve the
  shared card. Only after the config can express R/H/E, probable pitchers and
  ladder pills. **This is the product decision** — treat it as a migration onto
  the generic path, not a choice between two MLB implementations.

Two invariants to encode in the config so today's bugs become structurally
impossible:

1. A tab's `id` **must equal** its panel's `data-panel-id`. NCAAF's mismatch
   should not be findable again.
2. A tab renders **only when its `required_field` is present**. NFL's empty
   Props tab and soccer's blank game-lens region are both "MLB-shaped slot, no
   data" — configuration decides that, not markup.

Legitimate differences that stay differences, all expressible in config:
soccer's draw and competition/leg; MLB's probable pitchers and R/H/E;
basketball's quarters; NCAAF's coverage/publication tier. **None justifies a
second stylesheet.**

## Do now, independent of everything

- **Tabular figures.** `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` on the numeric card
  classes in `dense_cards.css`, `cards_exact.css`, `cards_source.css`,
  `cards-parity.css`. Four lines. These are the surfaces that update on a 30 s
  cycle with proportional digits, so the jitter is visible on every poll.
- **Touch targets and ARIA, bundled.** Raise mobile tab targets to 44 px (every
  sport fails on height today; MLB is 60×28 with 9 px labels) and add real
  `role="tablist"`/`"tab"`/`"tabpanel"`, `aria-controls`, arrow-key navigation.
  Drop the redundant Enter/Space handler — `<button>` already does that, and it
  currently runs `activateTab` twice.

## Explicitly not now

- **Per-fork typography cleanup.** 32 distinct font-size values platform-wide
  with no scale, and the same class rendering 1.6–1.78× larger on one sport than
  another. Real, but fixing it per-fork means doing it four times and throwing it
  away at I5. Wait for one stylesheet. (Tabular figures are the exception above:
  four lines, high visibility, cheap to redo.)
- **Backporting NBA's 99 live-lens lines into WNBA.** Superseded by I3.
- **Anything in `board-ui-freshness-slip-books`' file set** until that lane
  closes.
