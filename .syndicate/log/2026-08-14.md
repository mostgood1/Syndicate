
## session: layer2-freshness — lane `layer2-board-freshness`

### What the session was asked
Find the cause of the refresh-worker memory build-up, and make the intelligence
engine that feeds the Layer 2 board fast enough that the board is fresh and does
not carry candidates that are no longer bettable.

### VERIFIED (production, refresh-worker, live commit `2e4e2544` re-read in the
same run that took the readings)

**Board freshness — window 2026-08-14 11:39-14:39Z (3h), 29,604 log rows:**
- `MEMORY_GUARD_ABORT stage=pre_source_state_fingerprint` **146** vs **5**
  completed board builds. **96.7% of board cycles are refused before any work.**
  Every other abort stage: 0. `OVERVIEW_STOPPED_FOR_MEMORY next_sport=mlb`: 3.
- Longest stretch with no `LAYER2_SHORTLIST` line: **104.7 min**
  (12:44:20 -> 14:29:00Z). Rebuild gaps n=4: min 2.1 / p50 7.8 / max 104.7 min.
- The refusing guard is `_MIN_SAFE_MEMORY_HEADROOM_BYTES` (1900MB), whose own
  comment sizes it for `build_intelligence_overview`'s ~1.9GB transient. It
  refuses the WHOLE publication (`intelligence_state.py`, the
  `pre_source_state_fingerprint` branch), so the Layer 2 shortlist dies with it.
- **Layer 2 does not consume what that guard protects.** On 3 of the 5 completed
  builds `CANDIDATE_POOL_READY count=0` while `LAYER2_SHORTLIST` on the SAME
  cycle returned `rows=256 considered=13665 sports=[mlb,nfl,soccer,wnba]`.
- Layer 2 stage cost, 4 builds 14:43-14:51Z: **14-27s, +27..181MB container.**
  Against a 1900MB floor.
- Legacy `candidate_collection_with_fallback` BUILD_SPAN: n=5, p50 0.00s,
  max 260.28s, **sum 498.7s** over the 3h. The 0.00s builds are the broken ones
  (empty overview), consistent with the note already in
  `collect_candidates_with_fallback_merge`.

**Memory — window 11:45-14:15Z (2.5h, pre-reboot, boot-segmented):**
- `anon` **2620.1 -> 2439.6MB**. It did NOT ratchet in this window. It
  oscillates ~1400-2800MB around a high plateau. **The 08-13 "leak in progress"
  regime is not what was happening today.** The problem today is the PLATEAU:
  p50 ~2200-2800 against the 2196MB the guard needs (4096-1900).
- Container at peak 3830.1MB / 93.5%; `accounted_rss` 2468.5MB across 6
  processes; main worker pid 39 at **2236.98MB**; `memory_reclaimable` 1428.0MB
  (file cache), `unreclaimable` 2402.7 ~= `anon` 2393.5.
- Dominant repeating allocation, by CONTAINER_MEMORY stage-transition deltas
  (3,427 samples): MLB **`board_contract`** (n=1,022 begin->games_normalized
  transitions, ~1 per 8.8s) and **`cards_context`** (n=127, ~1 per 71s), a
  sawtooth of roughly +637MB (`cards_context_end -> board_contract_begin`)
  against -555MB (`board_contract_games_normalized -> cards_context_begin`).
- **The board build is NOT the retainer** — it ran 5 times in 3 hours while
  `anon` stayed high the whole time.
- A deploy landed at **14:22:32Z (`2e4e2544`, trigger=api, another session)**
  and restarted the worker. `anon` fell to 244MB and builds resumed at ~2min
  cadence. That is the reboot, not a fix — `state.md` already records
  "restarts clear it and prove nothing".
