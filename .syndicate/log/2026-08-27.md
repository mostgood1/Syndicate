## 20:0x–20:1xZ — `#590` the rail's card-head label; lane `rail-league-label` opened, shipped, verified, CLOSED

User, after `#589` shipped: "yes fix the SOCCER vs LA LIGA label too."

accident. `group.sport` is `item.sport || item.sport_slug` from the FIRST row

**Measured BEFORE writing it**, which is the reason reading `league_display` is
safe rather than merely nicer: populated for **213 of 213** soccer chips across

whose chips carry no league. Deployed web `0e964af8`, live `20:07:37Z`.

**The report understated it by two orders of magnitude.** It read as one odd
card; only **2 of 213** soccer cards carried a league, so the other 211 were the
ones mislabelled.

**THE METRIC WENT BLIND BETWEEN TWO PAYLOADS TWELVE MINUTES APART, AND THAT IS
THE ENTRY WORTH KEEPING.** The census first flagged "a sport showing a BARE
SPORT label beside a league". On the 19:5xZ payload the pre-change template read
`SOCCER=211 LA LIGA=2` and it fired. By 20:0xZ the league-labelled steam/prop
rows had drained off the board; the SAME pre-change template read a uniform

the post-deploy payload would have produced a control that looked clean and a
pass that proved nothing — `learnings.md` 2026-08-20, again, and it was caught
only because both payloads were kept and both templates were run against both.

Replaced with an invariant anchored on the SOURCE, not on the spread of rendered
labels: *if a card's chip carries a league, the card must not render the bare
sport.* Served `213 / 0` on both payloads; control `213 / 213` and `213 / 211`.

**THREE INSTRUMENT DEFECTS IN TWO FIXES TONIGHT, ALL READING HEALTHY.** A
duplicate detector that reused the code under test; a harness that hand-copied
