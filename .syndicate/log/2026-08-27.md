## VERIFIED

**The NCAAF `pace` block was never merely absent — it was pinned WRONG.**
`drive_priors._pace_index` falls through to a hardcoded 24.0 s/play, so every

    no pace block (today)   +0.400   6.31 plays/drive   151.6 s/drive

~18% too fast for an average team. `drive_success_probability` is IDENTICAL

play count — which is what makes a totals attribution possible at all.

**Reachability, end to end (snapshot -> payload -> priors):** Alabama/Auburn
+0.400 -> +0.141, Ohio State/Michigan -> -0.100, Baylor/TCU -> +0.466.
Previously all three were identical. Not a constant shift — Baylor/TCU comes
out FASTER than the old default while Ohio State/Michigan is much slower.

**Data quality, and the guard earned its place.** `pace_index_at_clamp_pct` is
printed every run and read 0.0% on 2025 but 13.3% on 2024, which is how the
CFBD defect was found at all rather than by inspection.

    2024  reported `elapsed` IMPOSSIBLE (>15 min) on 1.39% of drives
    2025  impossible on 0.00%, but the two methods disagree on 16.5%

Final rule: prefer `elapsed` while plausible, fall back to the derived clock
only when it is not, drop when neither survives, plus a physical guard (no
drive averages >60s of clock per play). Result: 2024 mean 26.270 sd 2.141,
2025 mean 26.267 sd 2.125, both 0.0% clamped. **Two independently-corrupted
seasons landing within 0.003 s/play is the evidence the rule is right.**

## A BELIEF I HELD AND THE DATA OVERTURNED

I concluded from 2024 that CFBD's `elapsed` was corrupt and switched to
deriving elapsed from the game clock ALWAYS. That was WRONG and shipped in
`3cede302` before being corrected in `1b463873`. In 2025 the DERIVED value is
the bad one:

    P1->1 start 15:00 end 0:00 plays=3  reported 86s  derived 900s

`endTime` 0:00 is a MISSING-DATA SENTINEL, not a real clock; `startPeriod: 0`
also occurs. Deriving always would have replaced good 2025 data with
fabricated slow drives. **A defect measured in one season did not generalise
to the next, and I generalised it anyway.**

Two further bugs were mine, found offline and unit-pinned: `_clock` returned 0
for an empty dict, so a missing `endTime` derived `start - 0` and inflated to a
full period — FABRICATING the very outliers the derivation existed to remove;
and college overtime has no game clock, so P4->P5 derived `start + 900`.
