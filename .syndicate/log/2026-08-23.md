## Mistakes made and corrected in-session

- **I reported NFL projection coverage "fell 100% -> 10%" as a regression. It is
  not.** Nothing was displaced (275 rows against a 400 cap) and the grid join is
  healthy (`considered=1427 projected=1021`, 71.5%). The unprojected live rows
  are unprojected **by design**: `nfl_game_projections.py` applies
  `live_edge_policy` because NFL has no live re-sim — "a pregame full-game total
  priced against a market that has already watched 55 minutes of football is not
  an edge, it is the score." The guard fired 248 times. Reporting it as a
  regression would have sent someone to fix a safety feature.
- **I verified the bet-slip fix by reading `data-collapsed="true"` off the SLIP.**
  True, and not the question — the user said *side rail*, and the rail is a
  separate default. The measurement matched my change instead of the complaint.
- **My first shed guard used `keep >= original`** and a test caught it shedding
  the board to ZERO rows — fresh and empty, worse than frozen. Correct condition
  is `base >= target`.
- **Three tests in `test_layer2_imminence_floor.py` were broken by MY earlier
  100->400 cap raise** and I had missed the file: a 160-row pool no longer
  exceeded the budget, so the starvation the file exists to reproduce stopped
  happening — exactly what its own docstring warned about. Now sized off the
  limit, verified at 100 and 400.
- **Two of my 18 new budget-test assertions were wrong about the arithmetic
  while the allocator was right.** Corrected in the test bodies with the
  reasoning, not quietly.
