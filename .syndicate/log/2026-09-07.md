**BUT THE ALARM WAS OVERSTATED, and the correction is the useful part.**

## mlb-ledger-segment-visibility — the ledger could not see the segment we bet

**Lane:** `mlb-ledger-segment-visibility` (session 3492626c). Landed `16b7ad1f`.

### What was actually wrong

Not "the ledger is segment-blind". The ledger's DENOMINATOR was truncated.
`attach_live_gamelines` refuses in two places relative to
`row["live_gameline"] = block`: refusals BELOW it land in the ledger as
`priceable: false` rows, refusals ABOVE it (segment, no-hit, stale quote) never
got a block and vanished entirely. So `14,003 of 27,249 priceable (51.4%)` was a
rate over the post-attach population, presented as a rate over the live one.

### Verified, on production

- Seven MLB ledger days pulled via `/api/ops/artifacts/export`, **28,763
  records: every single one `segment=full`.** No first5, first3 or first1.
- The only `withheld_reason` values present were the three post-attach ones
  (`prob_interval_swamps_edge`, `no_two_sided_market_price`,
  `live_resim_published_no_distribution_for_this_market`). Not one pre-attach
  reason — which is the falsification test for the lane, and it held.
- The join's own coverage counters, `book_grid_2026-09-0{5,6}.json`:
  `segment_is_not_full_game` **25 of 29** and **34 of 41** rows considered
  (86%, 83%). It is the largest category of live rows there is.
- `sources_seen` on 2026-08-23: `live_mc` 20 against `segment_projection` 40 —
  the lens publishes segment lanes at TWICE the re-sim count and
  `accepted_sources: ["live_mc"]` filters them out at index time. So there are
  two independent segment guards, not one, and each is protecting against the
  MISMATCH; neither protects against a matched segment pairing.

### What shipped

A refusal record, under a SEPARATE key (`REFUSAL_KEY = "live_gameline_refusal"`),
emitted by `build_records` after every `live_gameline` record.

- **The guard is untouched.** Its measurement stands: a full-game projection
  priced against a first-inning market gave +42.43 pp of edge that was entirely
  an artifact of the mismatch (SD @ CLE, 2026-08-16). Counting is not pricing.
- **Separate key = zero blast radius.** `layer2_board` copies
  `row["live_gameline"]` onto the candidate and `live_gameline_score` scores it;
  a refusal under that name would put an empty live block on every first5 board
  row. Nothing but the ledger reads the new key.
- **Bounded by dedup, deliberately.** `_moved` compares exactly the four fields
  a refusal record holds constant, so it is written once per (game, segment,
  market, line, book set) per day. Carrying the segment market's own de-vig
  would be useful but MOVES, and `append_records` stops writing for the REST OF
  THE DAY at `_MAX_RECORDS_PER_FILE`; MLB already wrote 8,070 rows on
  2026-08-21 against that 20,000 cap while segment rows outnumber full-game rows
  ~5:1. Ordering refusals last means `records[:500]` drops one before it drops a
  priceable row.
- Fine-grained game-state fields are NULL, not stale: dedup writes at first
  sighting and never refreshes, so an inning stamped there would sit frozen
  while reading as current.
