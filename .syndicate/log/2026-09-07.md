### Four failed confirmation attempts, and what each one taught

**1 — I built a narrower copy of a guard that already existed.** Pre-screened
for an in-flight MLB sim by grepping the last `ALL_PROCESS_MEMORY` line for
`mlb_daily_sim`. At 05:02Z that string was absent, so my check said clear.
`deploy_preflight.py`, reading the actual process table, said
`HOLD: 5 job(s) in flight` — `run_mlb_daily_sim_job.py`, `daily_update.py
--workflow ui-daily`, and three vendor children, none of whose cmdlines
contained my substring. Cost: flag armed and the claim held on a contended
service for 33 minutes for a deploy that was never going to go. The fix is not
a longer substring list, it is not having a second instrument.

**2 — armed before acquiring.** Set `SYNDICATE_SEGMENT_REGRADE_APPLY_ON_BOOT=1`
and only then tried to acquire. The claim was held by `ncaaf-live-resim-wire`,
so five passes ran `set 1 -> acquire refused -> set 0`, ~1 second armed each.
No deploy fell in any window (theirs finished 06:04:24Z, first arm 06:25:48Z),
so nothing rode it. Ordering was still wrong: the flag was `1` while another
session held the sole right to deploy. Fixed to claim-first, arm-second,
disarm-before-release.

**3 — a pinned target went stale into a revert.** `TARGET=5876bbc9` was chosen
at 04:25Z because it was then live. At 06:04:24Z the peer deployed `8f647bbb`.
Measured: `5876bbc9` IS an ancestor of `8f647bbb`, and not the reverse — so the
pinned target had become a rollback of their work. Killed the watcher.

**BUT THE ALARM WAS OVERSTATED, and the correction is the useful part.**
`render_deploy.py:136-156` already re-reads the live sha AT DEPLOY TIME and
refuses unless the target is a descendant (`--allow-rollback` is the deliberate
escape; its docstring cites the 2026-08-14 incident of exactly this shape). My
watcher goes through that entrypoint with no `--allow-rollback`, so it would
have hit `REFUSING: ... would ROLL BACK production`, returned 2, and my loop
treats rc!=0 as fatal — disarm, release, no deploy. **It would have failed
loudly. It was never armed to revert.** Raised by `ncaaf-live-resim-wire`;
re-derived here rather than taken.

**4 — inert by construction.** Rebuilt around `target == live` to "isolate the
env flag as the only variable". `render_deploy.py` returns 2 on exactly that
(`ALREADY live -- nothing to deploy`), and preflight independently reports
`HOLD: 8f647bbb is already contained in live 8f647bbb -- the deploy is
redundant`. It could never have fired. The instinct was also contrary to
CLAUDE.md's own doctrine: deploys are meant to be CUMULATIVE and roll-forward
from `main` is the sanctioned shape. I was treating the rule's purpose as an
obstacle.

### Withdrawn suggestion
