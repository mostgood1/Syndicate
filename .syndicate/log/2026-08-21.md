### The one decision left, and it must not be made silently

Phase 3(b) needs `liveModelProbOver`. The sim ladder is the PREGAME
distribution — `P(final >= line)` from tip-off — while a live prop needs
`P(final >= line | current, minutes played)`, the distribution of the REMAINDER.
Scaling the full-game shape (mean by `m/min_mean`, sd by `sqrt(m/min_mean)`) is
the standard assumption and is UNMEASURED HERE. Grade it and
`prob_std_err(p, simCount)` applies honestly at `simCount: 100` — the same
machinery MLB's 120-sim game lines already use.
