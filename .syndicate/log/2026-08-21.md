### What it is

`/nfl/fantasy` plus `/nfl/api/fantasy/{projections,draft-board}`: ESPN-scoring
season and weekly projections for QB/RB/WR/TE/K/DST, with a value-over-
replacement draft board. Opportunity is modelled as a CLOSED SYSTEM per team —
each player is a share of his team's pass/carry/target/kick pool and the shares
normalise so the season pool is exactly consumed, which is what makes roster
change work without special cases. Efficiency shrinks to a position mean via
`player_stats.shrink_count_mean`, the shrinkage already tuned for the prop model.

Environment comes from POSTED MARKET LINES, deliberately not from smartsim2 —
`state.md [football-smartsim2]` measured it strictly dominated by the close
(w = -0.028, 751 OOS games).

### VERIFIED

- **Held-out 2025, one common 266-player set for every method:** season MAE
  49.41 → **47.67**, spearman 0.7058 → **0.7392**; per-game 3.68 → **3.56**,
  0.6138 → **0.6337**. Better on all four; rank correlation better at every
  position.
- **The test ran four times and FAILED once, and the fail was reported** rather
  than tuned away. Fixing the defect it exposed (availability compression)
  produced the current pass.
- Input checklist exits 0; 21/21 own tests; `/nfl/fantasy` and both JSON routes
  200 and rendered on a local instance; week-5 byes correctly drop KC and CAR.
- Per-game usage lines sum EXACTLY to season totals, 0 mismatches 2022-2025.
- The 4 failures in `pytest -k "nfl or artifact_publisher or publish"` (917
  passed) reproduce identically on a clean `origin/main` worktree — checked.

### BELIEVED BUT UNVERIFIED

- **Nothing observed on Render. Nothing deployed. Nothing pushed to `main`.**
- The 2026 projections are a forecast; only the 2025 backtest is evidence.
- News-layer keyword weights are reasoned, not fitted. It ships off.

### Dead ends — measured, do not re-run

- **Role SHARE curve**: built to fix visibly-wrong output, then fitted to ZERO.
  Pulling teams toward the league-average split was the single largest accuracy
  loss in the engine. Its twin, the GAMES curve, is load-bearing.
- **"The games curve is survivor-conditioned"**: it is, but the fix is a NO-OP —
  teams already field 11 players with carries and 15 with targets against a
  curve depth of 8, so zeros never reach the modelled ordinals.
- **"In-season callups take share the preseason roster cannot see"**: 0.1-1.8%
  of team opportunity across 2023-25.
- **Six of nine, then seven of eleven, swept constants**: noise-level, left
  UNFITTED at defaults rather than banked as tuned.

### The three lessons worth carrying

1. **A `max(timestamp)` inside a season-scoped artifact is a hindsight leak, and
   it flatters.** Already in `learnings.md`.
2. **A grid's width is not its effect size.** `season_recency_weights` spanned
   1.43 MAE, but ~1.35 of that was "one season vs more than one", which the
   default already cleared; the "winner" beat the incumbent by 0.0003.
3. **Do not optimise the sub-quantity.** The availability fix is a WORSE
   predictor of games and a BETTER predictor of season points, because points
   are a PRODUCT of games and rate and a compressed factor biases the product.
   A games-MAE sweep alone would have rejected it.

### Owed

Nothing is on Render. If deployed: build usage/news/input-report artifacts ON
THE WORKER, and set `SYNDICATE_NFL_FANTASY_USAGE_STRICT=1` on web so a
request-path pbp parse fails loudly instead of running slowly. A new usage FIELD
needs `build_nfl_fantasy_usage.py --force`, not just a deploy.
