### `#632` — the non-malloc anon is pymalloc, and it does not grow

Built and deployed the anon partition (three deploys: `ad218a3e`, `2211d59b` to
recalibrate an overlap bar that false-tripped in production, `cc598278` to turn
the flag back off). Claim held throughout, released after.

**Answer.** `anon = glibc_malloc + pymalloc_arenas + so_private_dirty +
main_thread_stack`, closing on 50/50 mature readings. The non-`malloc` part is
CPython's pymalloc arenas at **162-165 MB/worker** — mmapped by CPython, so
`mallinfo2` was structurally blind to them. `.so` private-dirty is exactly
`4.4 MB` and the main stack `0.1 MB`, both flat for 66 minutes.

**And the hypothesis was half wrong in the useful direction.** I had said pymalloc
"becomes the growth driver" once glibc plateaus. Measured: `+0.0 MB` over 31
mature minutes on BOTH workers. It is a large constant, not a leak. That claim was
speculation stated as a finding, and I withdrew it before the measurement rather
than after only because the window happened to run first — the process that
matters is that it was never measured when I said it.

reproduce UPDATE 23's growth at all (anon `-5.8`/`+15.3` vs `+42.4`/`+26.9`), so
growth on web is INTERMITTENT. One stable window is not evidence that growth
