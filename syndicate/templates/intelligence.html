How this board ranks. Rows are scored on expected value against the no-vig market price, plus how much of that value survives price shopping. Our simulation contributes as well, but it is capped at 1.5 EV points — enough to order rows that are otherwise close, and to decide which side of a market we prefer, and it can carry a row across our value floor onto the board — but only by that much, so it can rescue a marginal price and never a materially bad one. A row our model loves by 40 points earns no more than one it likes by 12. So this is a price-led board that the simulation breaks ties on and nudges the margins of, not a list of bets our model picked. The sim disagrees tag marks rows our own model rates worse than the price implies. Nothing here has been graded against outcomes yet — the weights are a stated prior, not a measured finding, and the Score column takes itself apart on hover so you can disagree with it.
