How this board ranksPrice first, the simulation nudges, stale prices sink. Tap for the details and the tag key.
1Price firstEvery row is scored on its expected value against the no-vig price across all books, plus how much of that edge survives shopping for the best price.
2The sim nudges, it doesn't decideOur simulation can add or take away at most 1.5 points. That breaks ties and picks which side we prefer — it can't turn a bad price into a play.
3Stale or thin prices sinkOld quotes, a single book, or a price that has moved away from us all lower the score.
4Hover any Scoreto see how many points came from price, sim, freshness and movement.
Sim tags sim disagrees our model rates this side worse than the price implies sim contradicts our model thinks the other side of the line is more likely sim off-scale our model is so far from the market (15+ points) that it is set aside; the row ranks on price alone sim, no edge our model has a view but there is no two-sided market to price it against sim, in noise our model's gap to the market is smaller than the simulation's own noise sim, average only our model projects an average here, not a probability at this line sim ± our model returned 0% or 100% — an instrument reading, not an opinion no sim view no model for this market — ranked on price alone No tag changes the score.
