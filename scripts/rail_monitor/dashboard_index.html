Board, shortlist and staked bets: results

From the fleet's daily model scorecard, which grades every line against its final result. Each day's value covers the 28 days before it, so the lines move slowly. Whole board is every priced line; published is what the Layer 2 shortlist put on the board; suggested is the subset the board marked as suggested (graded at the first sighting of each line, so it sees about half of the lines suggested later in a day, from 10-13); staked is what the portfolio actually bet, graded as paper at a flat stake (the live-money book is on Portfolio Watch). ROI is profit per unit staked. Predicted EV is what the model expected; price vs fair is the price against the no-vig fair.

Cheat sheet picks

From the cheat sheets' own daily review (official record). ROI is profit per unit at a flat stake. "Against its fair" is hits minus the probability the price implied, in points; "against other lines" compares the section's picks with the same sheets' lines it did not feature (same sport, side and price band). Both carry a 95% range over games; a range that crosses 0 is not separable from the price. The review flags a record only at 30+ bets and |z| of 2 or more.

Optimization program: ranked levers

From the optimization session's program (lane product-optimization-program, 11-18 evening). Each lever is a per-line change; none withholds a sport, market or band. "Clears" means it beat noise on both bars (Benjamini-Hochberg at q 0.10 and leave-one-date-out), with 95% ranges clustered by game. Tier 1 is live money, tier 2 the board and paper book, tier 3 defects in the evidence itself. The headline: no live-money lever clears the bar yet (venue fees are 78% of the live loss); calibration does, because the model is over-confident everywhere it is measured.
